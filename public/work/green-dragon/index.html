Our Story

A Little Town
with a Big Heart

The Green Dragon Vape Lounge is a family-owned and operated business nestled right in the center of Honea Path. We're not just a store — we're a community gathering place.

Whether you're stepping into the world of vaping for the first time or you're a seasoned enthusiast, our knowledgeable staff will walk you through everything. We carry only American-made premium e-liquids, CBD products, and hundreds of devices.

We believe in superior price and service — and we'll learn your name, greet you at the door, and thank you for every visit.
