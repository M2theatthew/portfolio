Our Story

A Honea Path Institution

Since 1985, Lou & Perry's has been serving up hearty Southern comfort food to families, farmers, and friends right here in Honea Path, SC. We're the kind of place where everybody knows your name — and your usual order.

From our all-day breakfast and famous homemade onion rings to daily meat-and-three specials, everything is made with care and served with a smile. Cash or check only — we keep it old-school, just like the food.
