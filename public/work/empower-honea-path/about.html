About Us

Built by neighbors, for neighbors.

Empower Honea Path is the nonprofit engine behind the partnerships, grants, and events that keep our downtown moving forward.

Purpose

Tourism, economic growth, quality of life.

The purpose of Empower Honea Path is to work with public, private, and civic entities to promote tourism, economic growth, and to enhance the quality of life in the Honea Path area.

We do that by organizing the events that draw people downtown, connecting business owners with the resources to improve their storefronts, and building the kind of partnerships that make bigger opportunities, like our new membership in Main Street SC, possible.

"The future of Honea Path is looking brighter than ever."

Milestone

Officially part of Main Street SC

On Wednesday, February 5, 2025, Empower Honea Path met with Main Street SC and was thrilled to announce Honea Path's selection into the program. Main Street SC is dedicated to revitalizing South Carolina's historic downtowns. As a member, Honea Path now receives comprehensive technical assistance, expert training, and access to a supportive statewide network to help transform our downtown into a vibrant destination.
