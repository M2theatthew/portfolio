Façade Grant Program

Façade Flair: Enhancing Storefronts, Revitalizing Downtown Charm

As downtown Honea Path continues its post-pandemic recovery, the town, in partnership with Empower Honea Path, is offering mini-grants aimed at small but impactful storefront improvements.

$

$800–$2,500

Mini-grants awarded to help businesses refresh and enhance their storefronts.

Design Guidance
