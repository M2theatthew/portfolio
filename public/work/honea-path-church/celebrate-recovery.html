What Is Celebrate Recovery?

A place to be honest, and not be alone in it.

Celebrate Recovery is a Christ-centered recovery ministry built around a simple idea: healing happens in community, not in isolation. Whether you're working through addiction, grief, broken relationships, anxiety, or habits you can't seem to shake on your own, this is a place to bring it into the light — without shame and without judgment.

You don't have to identify with any particular struggle to belong here. Celebrate Recovery is for anyone who's ever needed to say "I'm not okay" and have someone respond with grace instead of a fix-it list.

What To Expect

Showing up is the hardest part. We've got the rest.

Step One

Show Up

Come as you are, Thursdays at 6:00 PM. No sign-up required, no experience necessary — just come through the door.

Step Two

Large Group

We start together with worship and a short teaching, grounding the evening in scripture and shared honesty.

Step Three

Find Your People

From there, smaller groups give space to talk, listen, and be known — at whatever pace feels right for you.

You're welcome here, every Thursday at 6.

No forms, no cost, no pressure to share more than you're ready to. Just come.
