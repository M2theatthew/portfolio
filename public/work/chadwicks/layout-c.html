Our Story

Lynn Chadwick opened these doors. They haven't really closed since.

It started simple: cook real food, charge a fair price, treat people right. Over 20 years later, that hasn't changed a bit.

We're a classic Southern meat-and-three. Come down the line, pick what looks good, find a booth. The board changes daily — follow Facebook so you know what's waiting.
